About this tool

Calculate your current pregnancy week, trimester and key milestones from your LMP date. This tool gives a general estimate based on standard health formulas — it's a starting point for understanding your numbers, not a diagnosis or medical advice.

How to use it

  1. Enter your details into the fields above — the calculator updates instantly as you type.
  2. Double-check units (metric or imperial) match what you're entering.
  3. Press calculate to see your result and any relevant health ranges.
  4. Re-run with different numbers to see how changes affect the outcome.

Common use cases

  • Getting a quick estimate before a doctor's appointment.
  • Tracking changes over time as your circumstances change.
  • Understanding what a result actually means in practical terms.
  • Comparing your numbers against general health guidelines.
