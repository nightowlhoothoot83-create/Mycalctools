Frequently Asked Questions

The Low Income Tax Offset can reduce income tax for eligible lower-income Australian residents. The maximum offset is $700, with the amount reducing as taxable income increases.

A simplified Medicare levy estimate is included. The actual levy can be reduced or exempt depending on taxable income, family income, dependants, SAPTO eligibility and other circumstances, so confirm the final amount with the ATO.

About this calculator

This calculator provides a general FY2026-27 Australian resident income tax estimate using the legislated resident tax brackets and LITO rules encoded in the tool. It also shows a simplified Medicare levy estimate, which should not be treated as a final Medicare assessment.

Use the calculator for rough take-home-pay and budgeting scenarios. It does not collect enough information to account for every tax offset, Medicare reduction or exemption, surcharge, study-loan obligation, residency issue or personal circumstance.

How to use Australian Tax Calculator

  1. Enter gross annual income and any deductions you reasonably expect to claim.
  2. Add deductible personal super contributions only where they are relevant to your situation.
  3. Press calculate to see the estimated tax and take-home breakdown.
  4. Confirm important tax decisions with current ATO guidance or a registered tax agent.

When this page is useful

It can help with salary comparisons, household budgeting and quick tax scenarios where an approximate result is useful before obtaining formal tax advice.

Privacy and accuracy

The calculation runs in your browser. Tax law and thresholds can change, and individual circumstances can materially affect the final result. This calculator is a planning aid, not a tax return or professional tax advice.
