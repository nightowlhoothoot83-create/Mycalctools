Freelancing vs Salaried Employment in Australia

Independent work moves costs that are often hidden inside employment onto the contractor: unpaid leave, administration, software, insurance and gaps between projects. Some contractors are still entitled to super from a client depending on the arrangement, while many sole traders choose their own contribution. Treat the percentage above as a pricing allowance, not a legal ruling about a particular contract.

A sole trader generally reports business income in their individual tax return, but withholding, PAYG instalments, deductions and super obligations depend on the arrangement. The calculator does not calculate income tax. Check the ATO guidance or use a registered tax agent for your circumstances.
