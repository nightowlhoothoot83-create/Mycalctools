Child BMI Measurement Calculator

Calculate raw BMI from a child's height and weight, then use the official age-and-sex chart with a health professional.

Ages 0–5Raw BMI Only

Important distinction

This page calculates raw BMI only. It does not calculate a percentile, z-score, diagnosis or healthy-weight category for a child.

What this tool actually calculates

The result is weight in kilograms divided by height in metres squared. For children, that raw number cannot be interpreted like an adult BMI: age in months, sex and the appropriate growth reference all matter.

How to use it

  1. Use a recent measured height and weight rather than an estimate.
  2. Enter age in completed months and select the chart sex used by the WHO reference.
  3. Calculate to record the raw BMI alongside the measurement details.
  4. Take the measurements to a GP, paediatrician or child health nurse for interpretation.

Useful, limited use cases

  • Writing down consistent measurements before a child-health appointment.
  • Checking that height was entered in centimetres and weight in kilograms.
  • Recalculating a raw BMI after a clinician corrects a measurement.
  • Opening the correct official chart for a child aged up to 60 months.

Why this page does not invent a percentile

The WHO publishes separate BMI-for-age percentile and z-score tables for boys and girls from birth to five years. A real percentile calculation requires those age-specific reference values; it cannot be derived from BMI alone. This page therefore reports the arithmetic result and leaves growth-chart interpretation to the official reference and an appropriately qualified health professional.

How to Use It

  1. Enter your child's age, sex, height, and weight.
  2. Check that the measurements use centimetres and kilograms.
  3. The calculator returns age, height, weight and raw BMI; it does not return a percentile.
  4. Open the official WHO BMI-for-age charts and tables, or ask a child health professional to plot the measurement.

What to take to an appointment

Record the measurement date, age in months, height, weight, who took the measurement and whether shoes or heavy clothing were removed. A series of careful measurements is usually more useful than one isolated number. Seek professional advice if you are worried about growth, feeding, weight change or development; do not restrict a young child's diet because of this calculator.

Children's BMI is interpreted by age and sex using a growth reference. The same raw BMI can mean something different at different ages, so an adult-style category would be misleading.
