About this tool

Calculate your Etsy fees, net profit and profit margin for any product listing. This tool gives a general working estimate — useful for quick decisions, though it's worth double-checking exact figures for anything contractual or tax-related.

How to use it

  1. Enter your figures — pricing, rate or platform details as relevant.
  2. Select the option or platform that matches your setup.
  3. Calculate to see the breakdown, including fees or margins where relevant.
  4. Run a few scenarios to see which option works out best.

Common use cases

  • Pricing a product or service before listing it.
  • Working out your real take-home after fees and costs.
  • Comparing platforms or pricing structures side by side.
  • Sanity-checking numbers before a client quote or invoice.
