Find the calculator you need

This directory brings together every MyCalcTools calculator and information page in one place. Browse by topic to find health and wellbeing estimates, Australian finance tools, kitchen conversions, date and lifestyle helpers, business calculators, environmental planning tools and everyday unit converters.

Each link opens the calculator directly. No account or app download is required. If you are unsure which calculator applies, start with the category that best matches your question and read the explanation on the tool page before entering your details.

Popular calculator categories

  • Health: BMI, calorie, pregnancy, ovulation, sleep, water intake and growth tools.
  • Finance: loans, mortgages, tax, pay, superannuation, HECS-HELP and cost-of-living estimates.
  • Kitchen: recipe scaling, cups-to-grams, pan sizes, air fryer timing and boiled egg guidance.
  • Lifestyle and practical maths: age, dates, fuel costs, tips, percentages, concrete and scientific calculations.
  • Business and eco: freelancer rates, digital products, Etsy fees, solar, rainwater and food-forest planning.

Using results responsibly

Calculator results are estimates based on the values and assumptions shown on each page. Check that units, dates, rates and location settings match your situation. For medical, financial, tax, legal or other important decisions, verify the result with an official source or suitably qualified professional.
